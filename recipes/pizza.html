Description

Homemade pizza is a delicious dish made with a crispy crust, flavorful tomato sauce, melted cheese, and your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Bell peppers
  • Onions
  • Olives
  • Italian herbs

Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and roll it into a round shape.
  2. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings.
  4. Sprinkle Italian herbs over the pizza.
  5. Bake the pizza until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
  6. Allow it to cool slightly, then slice and serve.
